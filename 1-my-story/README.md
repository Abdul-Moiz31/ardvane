# My story — attacked three times in five weeks

I'm a software developer. Between early September and October 2026 my laptop and my GitHub account were
hit by the **PolinRider** worm — part of the North Korean "Contagious Interview" operations that target
developers — **three times**. I reinstalled my Mac, revoked my tokens, rewrote my repositories' history,
tried scanners, read every article I could find… and it still came back.

This folder is the honest account of what happened, what I tried, what didn't work and what finally did,
so you can skip the weeks I lost.

**The short version:** the malware didn't just live on my laptop. It lived in my **repositories**, in my
**collaborators'** machines and in **credentials** it had already stolen. Every time I cleaned only one of those,
one of the others brought it back. It only stopped when I dealt with all of them at once — and put protections
in place so the next attempt gets blocked automatically. That's what this kit does.
